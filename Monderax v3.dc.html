Machined from
solid aluminium

One billet. No fasteners in the load path.

Nine hours on the mill turns a 34 kg block into an 8 kg shell. The GPU bracket, the fin stack and the feet are the same piece of metal, so heat leaves through the walls instead of being blown at your desk.

What the metal does.

Anodised, not painted, with 316L stainless fasteners and a conformal-coated board set — a tropical spec that survives 85% humidity for a decade. Every figure below is measured on the machine that ships, not on a prototype.
