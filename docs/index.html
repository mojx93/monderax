A machined frame
and two open walls

Fifteen litres. Ninety-six gigabytes. No cupboard required.

The frame is cut from 6082-T6 plate — one piece carrying the board, the card and the cooler. Both end walls are perforated on a 3 mm grid across 42% of their area and the long wall is tempered glass, so a 600-watt card pulls cool air straight through the machine rather than sipping it through a slot. Everything above the card is clearance for its power cable — 20 mm of it, which is why the machine is 246 mm tall and not 226.

What the metal does.

Anodised, not painted, with stainless fasteners and a filtered intake you can rinse in the sink. Every figure below is measured on the machine that ships, not on a prototype.
